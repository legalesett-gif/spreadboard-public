The Provider operates the SpreadBoard crypto-market research service at
`https://spreadarbitrage.ink` (the **Service**). The Partner operates the channel
or audience identified in the Deal Sheet and wishes to refer potential customers
to the Service.

## 1. Appointment

1.1 The Provider appoints the Partner on a non-exclusive, revocable basis to
promote the Service using the Partner's assigned referral link.  
1.2 The Partner is an independent contractor and has no authority to bind the
Provider, collect customer funds, give warranties for the Provider, or describe
the Service as an investment adviser, broker, exchange, custodian or trading
service.  
1.3 No minimum number of videos, referrals or sales is required unless added to
the Deal Sheet in writing.

## 2. Referral link and attribution

2.1 The Provider will create one cabinet and one unique link for the Partner. The
public link name is normally generated from the channel name; it is merely a
readable URL identifier and has no economic effect.  
2.2 A qualifying click may be remembered for up to 90 days. Attribution is fixed
to the first qualifying Partner when the customer registers.  
2.3 Subject to this Agreement, that attribution continues for later settled
renewals by the same customer, including manual prepaid renewals.  
2.4 The Partner must not alter, transfer, mask or automate the link, overwrite
another partner's attribution, use cookie stuffing, or create self-referrals,
duplicate accounts, misleading redirects or artificial traffic.

## 3. Customer offer

3.1 A qualifying new customer receives 20% off the first 30-day membership value
once.  
3.2 If the customer's first invoice prepays 90 or 365 days, only the equivalent
of one 30-day membership value is discounted. Later renewals are not discounted
unless the Provider agrees otherwise in writing.  
3.3 The offer cannot be transferred, combined, exchanged for cash or applied
retroactively.

## 4. Commission

4.1 The Partner earns 50% of subscription plan revenue actually received and
finally settled for an attributed customer, after any customer discount and
excluding invoice-identification cents, taxes, refunds, reversals, duplicate
payments and sums not belonging to the Provider.  
4.2 Commission is earned only after the corresponding customer payment is
confirmed. A registration, click, invoice creation or unpaid invoice does not
create commission.  
4.3 Valid attributed renewals continue to earn commission while this Agreement
remains in force. An ordinary pause of the public link stops new attribution but
does not erase valid existing attribution.  
4.4 The Provider may withhold or void commission reasonably connected to fraud,
self-referral, duplicate accounts, sanctions concerns, a refund, a payment
reversal, misleading promotion or a material breach by the Partner. The Provider
will record the reason in the affiliate ledger.

## 5. Hold, statements and payment

5.1 Each commission is held for seven days after settlement and then becomes
eligible for the next weekly payout batch.  
5.2 Payouts are made only in **USDT on Arbitrum One** to the wallet recorded by
the Partner in the cabinet. The Partner is responsible for providing a compatible
and accurate wallet address. A transfer to the saved address discharges the
Provider's payment obligation for that batch.  
5.3 The Partner must review each statement promptly and report a suspected error
within 30 days. The Provider will retain an invoice-level commission ledger and
the transaction reference for each paid batch.  
5.4 The Provider is not responsible for loss caused by an incorrect wallet,
unsupported network, compromised Partner account, loss of wallet access or a
blockchain event outside the Provider's reasonable control.

## 6. Promotion and mandatory disclosure

6.1 Every promotion must be truthful, balanced and consistent with the current
Service pages and pricing. The Partner must not promise profits, guaranteed
returns, risk-free trading, specific future performance or automatic execution.  
6.2 The Partner must clearly and conspicuously disclose the commercial
relationship. For a YouTube promotion, the disclosure must be spoken and shown
on screen near the start of the promotion and must also appear in the
description immediately before the referral link, for example: **“AD — paid
affiliate promotion. I receive a commission if you subscribe through this
link.”** A platform's paid-promotion tool may be used in addition, but does not
replace this disclosure.  
6.3 The Partner must clearly distinguish market research from investment,
financial, legal or tax advice and must not make claims that would require the
Partner or Provider to hold a licence they do not hold.  
6.4 The Partner must comply with applicable advertising, consumer-protection,
privacy, sanctions and platform rules, including the rules applicable where the
Partner and the audience are located.

## 7. Prohibited activity

The Partner must not use spam, unsolicited bulk messages, malware, bots, fake
engagement, paid-search bidding on the Provider's brand, impersonation,
misleading domain names, unlawful content, undisclosed endorsements, fabricated
results, customer credentials, or any content reasonably likely to damage the
Provider or its users.

## 8. Brand and intellectual property

8.1 During the term, the Provider grants the Partner a limited, non-exclusive,
non-transferable and revocable licence to use approved SpreadBoard names and
assets solely to perform this Agreement.  
8.2 The Partner must not modify the marks in a misleading way or imply ownership,
endorsement beyond this affiliate relationship, or exclusivity. All goodwill in
the Provider's marks belongs to the Provider.  
8.3 The Partner owns their original content, subject to the Provider's right to
link to or repost that content only with the Partner's permission.

## 9. Confidentiality and data

9.1 Each party must protect non-public commercial, technical and customer
information received from the other and use it only for this Agreement.  
9.2 The Partner receives aggregate and invoice-level commercial statistics but
does not receive customer email addresses, passwords, wallet credentials or
exchange credentials.  
9.3 Each party is independently responsible for its own privacy notices and data
processing. Neither party may sell or misuse personal data obtained through the
relationship.

## 10. Taxes, identity and compliance

10.1 The Partner is responsible for declaring and paying all taxes arising from
commission. The Provider may request reasonable identity, address, tax or
sanctions information before payment where required by law or a service
provider.  
10.2 The Partner represents that they are legally able to enter this Agreement
and receive USDT payments at the declared address.

## 11. Term, suspension and termination

11.1 This Agreement starts on the Effective Date and continues until either party
gives 14 days' written notice.  
11.2 The Provider may immediately suspend the link or cabinet while investigating
fraud, security, sanctions, unlawful promotion or material reputational risk.  
11.3 Either party may terminate immediately for a material breach that cannot be
cured, or that is not cured within seven days after written notice.  
11.4 On ordinary termination, new attribution stops. Properly earned, non-
disputed commission remains payable after the hold period. Existing customer
attribution continues only if the Deal Sheet says **Yes** below. Fraudulent or
materially misleading referrals do not survive termination.

## 12. Service and earnings disclaimer

The Service is provided as a research product and may change, be interrupted or
contain delayed, unavailable or third-party data. The Provider does not guarantee
customer conversions, commission, availability, trading outcomes or any minimum
income for the Partner.

## 13. Liability

Nothing excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded. Subject to that,
neither party is liable for indirect or consequential loss. Each party's total
liability under this Agreement is limited to the commission paid or payable in
the six months before the event giving rise to the claim, except for fraud,
wilful misconduct, misuse of confidential information or infringement of the
other party's intellectual property.

## 14. General

14.1 Neither party may assign this Agreement without the other's written consent,
except that the Provider may assign it with the Service or its business.  
14.2 Notices must be sent to the emails in the Deal Sheet.  
14.3 This Agreement and Deal Sheet are the entire agreement and may be amended
only in writing. If a term is unenforceable, the remaining terms continue. No
failure to enforce a term is a waiver.  
14.4 Electronic signatures and counterparts are permitted.  
14.5 This Agreement is governed by the law of **[England and Wales — confirm with
counsel]**, and the courts of **[England and Wales — confirm with counsel]** have
exclusive jurisdiction.
